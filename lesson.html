The Art of Paper Folding

Welcome to the beautiful world of Origami. Derived from the Japanese words ori (folding) and kami (paper), this ancient art form transforms a simple flat sheet of paper into a finished sculpture through folding and sculpting techniques.

Why Learn Origami?

Origami is more than just making paper animals. It is a relaxing and meditative practice that helps improve focus, hand-eye coordination, and spatial thinking.

By following the steps carefully, you are training your brain to understand geometry and patience. Plus, you get to create something beautiful out of nothing!
