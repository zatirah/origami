The Whale

Master the foundational folding techniques by creating this elegant sea creature.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Fold a square paper diagonally to make a crease, then unfold it.
Fold the top and bottom edges inward to meet the center line.
Fold the left point inward to round out the whale's head.
Fold the entire model in half along the center line securely.
Using scissors, make a small horizontal cut at the thin tail end.
Fold the cut sections upward and outward to form the tail fins.
